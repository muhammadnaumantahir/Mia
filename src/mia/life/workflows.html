Workflows

Chain Mia's internal actions or supported computer actions into one plan. Every step is previewed and revalidated before it runs. Computer, browser, terminal and desktop actions use the shared owner permission policy and exact one-time approvals; running or retrying a workflow requires your owner passkey. Unsupported capabilities fail closed. Pause and Cancel remain available as safety controls.

New workflow

For actions without a built-in form, enter the adapter arguments as a JSON object. The preview shows current risk and whether an owner approval is required. A workflow run cannot bypass the underlying computer permission grants or per-action approval.

A later step can use an earlier result by writing ${steps.1.item_id} in a field (step numbers start at 1). Up to 20 steps.
